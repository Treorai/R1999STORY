# Prologue

Pirate Regulus was on the APPLe ship, when she hears cannon shots, aiming at her ship. It was the **Manus Vindictae** Emblem.
Regulus had "kidnap" some people she thought was with them, and offered to let them free if they spare the ship, but turns out they were Investigators from the **Foundation**. Sonetto was looking from distance.  
Sonetto used a Flopy Disk to summon **Vertin**, at a forest. Vertin¹ is with the St. Pavlov Foundation, and her mission is to rescue the investigators, complete the registration of Regulus and return to the research center before the **Storm** arrives, in 50 minutes. Vertin use the Floopy Disk to find Regulus and the investigators. When she does, Sonetto casts an Incatation to save them.  
They were searching for Regulus because she was making unauthorized use of **Arcanum**, which is a violation of the Public Security Law. They require her to cooperate with the investigation and registration process. She agrees, and asks to see the Teleportation Floppy Disk. She is impressed that incantations can be recorded and read. They get ambushed by Manus Vindictae minions, and while Vertin and Sonetto defeat the rioters, regulus triggers the **Ritual** and disappear in the chaos.  
We discover that Vertin is the only one that can move freely in the storm.  

The investigators say Regulus is probably at West End near Kingly Court. Vertin and Sonetto go look for her. There, a newsboy is talking about the hero Pickles. He tells Vertin she might find Regulus at the Coffeehouse. There, they do find her, invisible. Sonetto casts an Incantation to split coffee on her, so we can look at her. Regulus don't want to join the foundation because the government would know her location.  
Sonetto engages in a fight against her, and break her arms. Vertin interfere and gives her a Misty Bubble Ball and tells Regulus to meet her at the bookstore on Oxford Street, without Sonetto's knowledge, and pretends to be pushed by Regulus, who bombs us and disappear.  
Sonetto and Vertin split up to look for Regulus. Vertin finds her hidig at a barber shop. Vertin say she won't make her go to the foundation, but asks her to go somewhere save with her.  

The collage of Richar Hamilton falls from the sky, splashing rain all over her. The storm starts. The edges of the buildings begin to melt, like the patterns in coming books. Sonetto goes back to the foundation.  
Regulus notices that the rain is rising from the ground. No one is acting about it, which means only a few people can see the Storm. Regulus can't believe that, saying "Why, I don't even have that little thing² anymore".  

At the last moment, Vertin manages to open the suitcase and push regulus inside. The times goes back, from 1965 to 1929.  

Vertin takes Regulus to the suitcase, *an ark*, *sailing in the storm*. Vertin is the **Timekeeper**. She keeps track of time in the outside world; the one that records the beginnings and ends of eras.  
Inside the suitcase, there is a wall with photos of Vertin and her life of date forward to 1999, her time. At the countdown to the millennium, before the clock struck twelve, time reversed, and they were sent back to the 80's, and then to the 60's - where they met Regulus.  
The suitcase protects everything inside from the storm, but when it comes to her friends, it failed. The Suitcase does not work in ordinary people.  
Sonetto and the foundation don't know about Vertin's experiment³.
