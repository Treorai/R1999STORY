## 1-1 Wretched Brats

Two kids find the suitcase. When they see Vertin getting out of the Suitcase, they call her of using dark magic. APPLe fights them¹. Vertin hides the suitcase behind a tree.  
Vertin gets back inside the suitcase to check on Regulus, but finds no one. Then, she hears crying from somewhere, a lake. The lake remained unchanged for ages, and an old spinning wheel too. This is where Vertin has never been. She finds the Spinning Wheel with a two dimensional thread. The ground is covered with rainwater from the storm. The water has no weight or humidily, just flows slowly and quietly, like time.  
She spins the Wheel, and pulls Sonetto. There, both Sonetto and Regulus black out, falling asleep.

### The opportunist and the sticky gum

> They found gum on the bottom of the knocked out kids' shoes. Based on the gravel mixed in the shoe, we can conclude that they have been walking at the "politically symbolic" floor of the Foundation. We find out that there are people who are **Mankind supremacists**. They are in the same side of the Foundation, but the foundation has no interest in giving them what they want. They even sent a little girl to go through the motions as a perfunctory response. She is nervous, and don't seem to be good in dealing with the slick politicians. The mission capsule grew hot in her clutched fist. On the other side of the square, another girl in a black suit is looking over here. She stood by the Foundation's air outlet, the best place for her. She had to perform another task she didn't like. At this moment, the capsule in the hand of the ad hoc representative of the Foundation dropped under the push of the crowd: this is a good chance. She feels a hot wind from Africa blow towards Trapani. Then, the whole of Sicily becomes dry, warm, and comes alive. The air smells tropical corals and wild oranges. This wind is never supposed to be part of this cold winter.
